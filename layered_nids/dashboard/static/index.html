Live capture

Capture this computer's own network traffic for a short time and check it with the signature and heuristic rules. The findings appear under Data: Live.

What this does and does not do

  • Windows shows an approval prompt for every capture. Nothing is captured until you click Yes.
  • Up to 120 seconds and 200,000 packets. The capture file is deleted right after the check; only the findings are kept.
  • Only the rules layer runs: known attack patterns and behaviour rules. The packet anomaly model needs about 70,000 clean packets of the same network to learn, so a short capture cannot feed it.
  • Only your own computer's traffic and what reaches it. Do not use it on networks where you have no right to record.
  • Nothing leaves this computer.
