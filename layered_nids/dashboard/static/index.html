Live capture

Record this computer's network traffic for a short time and check it against the signature and behaviour rules. Findings appear under Data: Live captures.

How live capture works
  • Windows asks for approval before every capture. Nothing is recorded until you click Yes.
  • A capture lasts up to 120 seconds. The recording is deleted right after it is checked; only the findings are kept, on this computer.
  • Live traffic is checked by the signature and behaviour rules. The anomaly model needs a longer baseline of the same network.
  • Only capture networks you are allowed to monitor.
